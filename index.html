how i work opinions, mostly held loosely
01
Ship the v1. Then make it better. A working thing in production teaches you more in a week than a clever branch teaches you in a month.
02
Vanilla JS until proven otherwise. Most of what people reach for React for can be done with a few hundred lines of plain JavaScript that won't be deprecated in two years.
03
Comments and READMEs are part of the work, not afterthoughts. I'll be the next developer on this code, and so will someone else.
04
The admin UI matters as much as the public site. Editors using it every day deserve good tools too.
05
AI is a tool, not a workhorse. I use it for boilerplate, rubber-ducking, and looking up syntax. The architecture and the decisions stay with me.
